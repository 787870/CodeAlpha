## 🛡️ 4. Key Risk Management Principles
*   **85/15 Core-Satellite Architecture:** Restricting high-beta cryptocurrency exposure to 15% ensures that a severe 50% crypto drawdown impacts total portfolio capital by only 7.5%, offset by equity dividends and ETF compounding.
*   **Systematic Execution via DCA:** Averaging cost basis across 4 to 6 monthly tranches eliminates lump-sum timing risk in volatile digital assets.
*   **Quarterly Rebalancing Governance:** Pruning satellite positions when crypto outperformance exceeds 20% of total portfolio value and redeploying profits into defensive equities and Nifty 50 BeES.
