## 💡 4. Strategic Rationale
* **Equities (50%):** Reliance serves as a conglomerate growth engine across retail and energy, while TCS and Infosys provide defensive IT earnings, robust cash flow, and a natural hedge against Rupee depreciation.
* **ETFs (35%):** Nifty 50 BeES captures systematic Indian economic expansion at low cost, while Gold BeES acts as a counter-cyclical damper against inflation and market drawdowns.
* **Crypto (15%):** Bitcoin provides institutional digital store-of-value exposure, and Ethereum captures smart contract infrastructure growth.

## 🛡️ 5. Risk Management & Rebalancing
* **Systematic Quarterly Rebalancing:** Resets asset weights back to target bounds (50/35/15) to maintain risk discipline.
* **Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA):** Deploys crypto capital across tranches to mitigate entry volatility.
